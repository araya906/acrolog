# Third-party material

## Reference photos (`img/photos/`)

Downscaled copies (500 px) of freely licensed photos from
[Wikimedia Commons, Category:Acroyoga](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Acroyoga), mostly CC BY-SA 2.0–4.0.
Each photo keeps its own licence. Author, licence and source page are listed in `src/photos.js` and shown under every
photo in the app. Regenerate with `tools/fetch_photos.ps1` (mapping in `tools/photos.json`).

## Videos and links (not bundled)

- Washing machine videos are links to YouTube/Instagram posts as listed on
  [Acropedia](https://www.acrolove.org/acropedia/) (AcroLove). They belong to their creators. The app embeds them only
  when the user taps Play (YouTube via the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player).
- Tutorial links point to the [Partner Acrobatics manual](https://partneracrobatics.com/manual/).
- Figure and flow names are common acroyoga terminology; descriptions and levels are AcroLog's own.

# Third-party material

## Reference photos (`img/photos/`)

Downscaled copies (500 px) of freely licensed photos from
[Wikimedia Commons, Category:Acroyoga](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Acroyoga), mostly CC BY-SA 2.0–4.0.
Each photo keeps its own licence. Author, licence and source page are listed in `src/photos.js` and shown under every
photo in the app. Regenerate with `tools/fetch_photos.ps1` (mapping in `tools/photos.json`).

## Acropedia library (`src/acropedia.js`)

Names, families, tags and video links of the washing machines listed on
[Acropedia](https://www.acrolove.org/acropedia/) (AcroLove, "Acropedia Project sheet"), generated by `tools/acropedia.ps1`.
Acropedia has no photos of its own: every entry points to a video on YouTube, Instagram or Facebook, which belongs to its
creator. AcroLog does not copy those videos or images. It links to them, embeds them only when the user taps Play
(YouTube via the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player), and shows YouTube thumbnails (from i.ytimg.com) only when
the user turns on "Show video thumbnails". Every library entry and tracked washing machine links back to its Acropedia page,
and the app asks users to credit the creators named in the original post.

## Other links (not bundled)

- Tutorial links point to the [Partner Acrobatics manual](https://partneracrobatics.com/manual/).
- Figure and flow names are common acroyoga terminology; descriptions and levels are AcroLog's own.

# AcroLog

Mobile app (installable web app, Android first) to track your acroyoga progress. Built the same way as VespaControl:
no build step, vanilla ES modules, IndexedDB on the device, no server, works offline.

- **Figures** (56): L-basing, standing, therapeutic, trios; levels Basic → Expert. For each figure, track your status
  **as base and as flyer**: Not started → Learning → With spotter → Solid → Mastered. Notes, goal star, edit or add your own.
  20 figures come with credited reference photos (Wikimedia Commons), many link to a Partner Acrobatics tutorial,
  and you can **add your own photos** (camera or gallery; shrunk to ≤1280 px and kept on the device).
- **Flows** (75): transitions, flows and 54 washing machines, as ordered steps of figures, tracked the same way.
  Washing machines include their **video** (from Acropedia, tap to play) and Acropedia page; their steps start empty on
  purpose: watch the video and add the steps the way your teacher breaks them down. Any flow can have a video link.
- **Acropedia library**: all 1,147 washing machines listed on Acropedia, searchable by name, family and tag, each with
  its video (tap to play) and Acropedia page. **+ Track** adds one to your flows. Optional YouTube thumbnails (off by default).
- **Friends, without accounts**: set your name, then **Share my progress**. It sends a link (WhatsApp, Signal…) that
  holds a compressed snapshot of your statuses and totals (optionally your last 10 sessions; never partner names or notes)
  after the `#`, so it never reaches any server. Opening a friend's link adds them; a **newer link replaces the older one**,
  and the app reminds you to *share an update* when your progress has changed since you last shared. For each friend:
  sessions practised together (matched to the name you write in Partners), what you are both ready to train (one bases,
  the other flies), what they can help you with and what you can help them with, and their recent sessions. Figures show
  which friends can do them. Partners from your log who haven't shared yet are listed too.
- **Automatic sync (optional)**: with the small sync server in `server/` deployed (free Cloudflare Worker + D1, see
  [server/README.md](server/README.md)) and its address in `src/config.js`, each person can turn on *Keep friends updated
  automatically*. Progress then uploads a few seconds after each change, and friends' updates arrive when the app opens,
  with no links to resend. Off by default; turning it off deletes your copy from the server. Backups carry your profile.
- **Log**: practice sessions with date, minutes, partners, what you practised, how it went and notes.
  "+ Practised today" on any figure adds it to today's session.
- **Progress**: sessions, hours, weekly streak, sessions per week, status per role, solid figures by level,
  "work on next" suggestions and your most frequent partners.
- **Instagram as a source**: every figure has a *Search #hashtag* button, and you can attach reel/post links.
  Once installed, Instagram → Share → **AcroLog** saves the link (Web Share Target); it waits in Figures until you attach it.
  Tracking parameters (`?igsh=`) are stripped. Previews load from Instagram only when you tap *Preview*.
- **Backup**: export / import a JSON file. Languages: English, Spanish, Basque (euskara). `node tools/check_i18n.mjs` checks that every language has every key.

## Run it

Serve the folder over HTTPS for installation (any static host, e.g. GitHub Pages), or locally:
`powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/serve.ps1 -Port 8123` and open `http://localhost:8123/`.

## Layout

- `index.html`, `css/app.css`: app shell.
- `src/app.js` UI, `src/catalog.js` starter figures and flows, `src/stats.js` pure progress helpers,
  `src/store.js` on-device storage and backup (friends included), `src/i18n.js` + `src/lang/*.js` texts.
- `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest` (with `share_target`): offline and installation. Bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` when shell files change.
- `src/sync.js` + `src/config.js` optional sync client; `server/` the sync Worker.
- `src/social.js` friend links (snapshot encode/decode/validate) and comparisons; `src/acropedia.js` library
  (generated by `tools/acropedia.ps1`).
- `src/media.js` video links and photo helpers; `src/photos.js` + `img/photos/` reference photos (generated by
  `tools/fetch_photos.ps1` from `tools/photos.json`). Credits and licences: [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
- `tools/icons.ps1` regenerates the PNG icons; `tools/serve.ps1` is a local static server.
